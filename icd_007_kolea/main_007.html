KOLEA

Interface Overview

The Kauhale On-Line Eligibility Assistance (KOLEA) System is the system of record for medical application and eligibility information. KOLEA interfaces with a number of other services and systems, sharing information to verify data and complete eligibility determinations. MQD contracts an Application System Integrator (ASI) to maintain and operate KOLEA on its behalf.

This section outlines the KOLEA delivery schedule, SFTP connection details, file notification process, and security/privacy considerations.
